Privacy Policy

Italiano Facile Storie · Last updated: 2026-09-26

1. Who is responsible for your data

Italiano Facile Storie (the "Service", Telegram bot @ItalianoFacileStorieBot) is operated by Enrique Alvarado Medina, an individual residing in Croatia ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Contact: italianofacile257@gmail.com

2. What data we process

We only process what is needed to run the Service:

  • Telegram identifiers: your Telegram chat ID and language code. We do not collect your phone number, contacts or location, and we cannot see your other chats.
  • Your messages to the assistant and its replies, which we use to understand the level, topic and length of the story you want. Only the most recent part of the conversation is kept.
  • Usage data: free-story counters, daily message counters, and the source of your first contact (for example the link or campaign you came from).
  • Story requests and generated content: the topic, level and length you ask for, and the resulting story text, images and audio. We also keep quality-check records (level, topic and corrections made to the text) to improve the language quality of the stories.
  • Subscription data: plan, remaining credits, renewal date and the Telegram payment transaction identifier. Payments are processed by Telegram; we never receive your card number or payment credentials.
  • Abuse-prevention records: usage counters and, where needed, a block list.

Please do not write personal or sensitive information (such as health, financial or identity data) in your messages: what you write is sent to the AI providers listed below.

3. Why we use your data and on what legal basis

  • To provide the Service you request (creating stories, images and audio, managing your free trial and subscription): performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fake accounts, enforce usage limits and improve the quality of the stories: our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • To keep payment and accounting records: compliance with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising. We do not take decisions that produce legal effects on you by automated means; usage limits (for example a daily message limit) are applied automatically to everyone.

4. Who receives your data

We use the following service providers (processors) that only process data on our behalf:

  • Telegram: messaging platform and payments in Telegram Stars.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH: hosting of our server and database, located in Finland (EU).
  • OpenAI: processes the text of your messages and story requests to understand them and to write and proofread the story text.
  • fal.ai: generates the story illustrations from text descriptions.
  • Microsoft Azure (Azure AI Speech): converts story text into audio.
  • ElevenLabs: converts story text into audio for subscribers.
  • GitHub: hosts this website.

We do not include your Telegram ID, name or phone number in the requests sent to these providers; only the text needed for each task.

5. International transfers

Some providers are located or process data outside the EU/EEA, for example in the United States. Where this happens we rely on the safeguards offered by these providers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, where applicable.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Conversation history: only the most recent messages are kept; older ones are deleted regularly.
  • Daily message counters: 30 days.
  • Quality-check records: up to 12 months.
  • Technical logs of our automation platform: up to 14 days.
  • Free-story counters and source of first contact: while the Service operates, because they are needed to enforce the free-trial limit and prevent abuse.
  • Subscription and payment records: as long as needed and as required by accounting and tax law.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, rectify it, have it erased, restrict or object to its processing, and receive a copy in a portable format. You can also withdraw any consent you have given.

To exercise your rights, write to italianofacile257@gmail.com and tell us your Telegram ID (you can find it with any "ID" bot) so we can identify your data. We will answer within one month. If you ask us to delete your data we will erase your conversation, quality-check records, message counters and source data. We may keep a minimal record (your Telegram ID and free-story count) to prevent abuse, such as resetting the free trial, and the payment records we are legally required to keep.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP, azop.hr) or with the data protection authority of your country of residence.

8. Children

The Service is not intended for people under 16. If you are under 16, please do not use it. If we learn that we hold data of a child under 16, we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data (access control, encrypted connections, spending and rate limits). No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. If changes are important we will tell you in the bot.
